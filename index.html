Isla Skills

An app store for robot skills. Each skill is a service a robot calls and pays per use, in USDC on Solana, with no human approving anything. 100% of each price goes to the skill's maker.

My contribution

Solo build: idea, design, front-end, server and tests.

  • Clean Sweep demo: Coco, a simulated cleaning robot, takes a photo of a room and pays a chain of three skills.
  • Litter Spotter (AI vision with Claude); when the AI is unsure, a real person reviews the photo and gets paid.
  • Cleanup Planner, and a 3D model of the villa where Coco drives to the room.
  • A robot wallet with a daily budget, working with Solflare, Phantom and Backpack. Real payments settled on Solana devnet.
  • JavaScript
  • Node.js
  • Serverless functions
  • Claude API
  • Solana
  • x402
  • three.js
  • Playwright

NASA Space Apps Challenge · Team project · Nov 2026

debris

Every piece left behind has a story. A web game for kids aged 7 to 12: they pick up litter, scan it with a phone, and each piece unlocks the story of a machine humanity left on the Moon, on Mars or in space.

My contribution

I built the playable web app from the team's designs and storyboard.

  • The round: scan a piece, hear its chapter, bin it, and earn a patch for a 3D explorer jacket.
  • An AI scanner that says which bin each piece goes in and warns kids not to touch dangerous items.
  • Two storytellers (one from Florianópolis' boi de mamão tradition), read-aloud, and an Impact screen.
  • English, Spanish and Portuguese. No accounts; photos are never stored. Lighthouse accessibility 100.
  • JavaScript

  • Node.js
  • Claude API
  • Web Speech
  • Web Audio
