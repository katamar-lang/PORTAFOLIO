Isla Skills

An app store for robot skills. Each skill is a service a robot calls and pays per use, in USDC on Solana, with no human approving anything.

Why I built it

Robots are learning new skills through software, but there's no way to pay for a skill each time a robot uses it. I wanted to show that machines can buy services from AI models and from people, automatically and fairly: 100% of each price goes to the skill's maker.

What I built

  • Clean Sweep demo: Coco, a simulated cleaning robot, takes one photo of a room and pays a chain of three skills.
  • Litter Spotter (AI vision with Claude) finds the litter; when the AI is unsure, a real person reviews the photo from their phone and gets paid.
  • Cleanup Planner turns the findings into a plan, and a 3D model of the villa shows Coco driving to the room.
  • Robot wallet with a daily budget and spending rules, works with Solflare, Phantom and Backpack.
✓ Real payments settled end to end on Solana devnet ✓ Mobile-first, tested on iPhone and Android sizes

NASA Space Apps Challenge
★ NEWTeam project · Nov 2026

debris

Every piece left behind has a story. A web game for kids aged 7 to 12: they pick up litter on Earth, scan it with a phone, and each piece unlocks the story of a machine humanity left on the Moon, on Mars or in space.

Why we built it

To connect two kinds of litter: the trash on our streets and beaches, and the 13 machines we left in space. Kids learn to clean up safely and discover real NASA history, so every pick-up feels like a mission.

What I built

  • The playable web app from the team's designs and storyboard: scan → story → bin it → earn a patch for a 3D explorer jacket.
  • AI scanner (adapted from Isla Skills' Litter Spotter) says which bin each piece goes in, and warns "Stop! Don't touch it" for dangerous items.
  • Two storytellers, including the Bull from Florianópolis' boi de mamão tradition, a read-aloud button, and an Impact screen: "Litter to the Moon".
  • Built for kids' privacy: no accounts, photos are never stored.
